##  My DSA Journey

My Data Structures and Algorithms journey began with a simple goal: to become a better problem solver by understanding concepts deeply rather than memorizing solutions. Since then, I have been consistently practicing DSA in Java, solving LeetCode problems, and documenting every solution in this repository as a record of my growth and learning.

Over time, I have built a strong foundation by working through fundamental topics such as Arrays, Hashing, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Prefix Sum, Intervals, Binary Search, and have recently started exploring Backtracking. Throughout this journey, I have solved a wide variety of problems ranging from beginner-friendly challenges to more logic-intensive questions, including algorithms like Kadane's Algorithm, 3Sum, Container With Most Water, Trapping Rain Water, Merge Intervals, Insert Interval, Non-overlapping Intervals, Binary Search on Rotated Arrays, Koko Eating Bananas, Subsets, Combination Sum, and Permutations.

Rather than focusing only on getting accepted solutions, I have dedicated my time to understanding why each algorithm works, identifying edge cases, comparing multiple approaches, and analyzing time and space complexity. I have also developed the habit of writing clean, readable Java code, improving my debugging skills, and learning common problem-solving patterns that can be applied across different DSA topics.

This repository is more than a collection of solved problems—it represents my commitment to consistency, discipline, and continuous improvement. Every solution added here reflects a concept learned, a mistake corrected, and another step toward becoming a stronger software engineer. As I continue progressing through my DSA roadmap, I aim to master advanced data structures and algorithms, strengthen my interview preparation, and build the confidence to solve increasingly challenging problems with efficient and optimized solutions.
